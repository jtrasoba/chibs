About LIFE²LAB

LIFE²LAB is a research group led by Jorge Trasobares at Universidad Complutense de Madrid. We investigate how electrical and electrochemical phenomena emerge, propagate, and can be engineered in living and bio-inspired systems.

Our research is rooted in molecular electronics, electrochemistry, and functional nanomaterials, and expands toward bioelectricity, applied mathematics, and living interfaces. We approach life as an electrically active, multiscale system, spanning molecules, nanostructures, cells, tissues, and hybrid bioelectronic devices.

LIFE²LAB was formerly known as CHIBS (Complex Hybrid Interfaces and Bioelectronic Systems).
